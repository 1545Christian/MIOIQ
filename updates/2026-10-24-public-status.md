# MIOIQ — Research progress and current boundaries (24 October 2026)

Deutsch: [Deutsche Fassung](2026-10-24-public-status_DE.md)

MIOIQ remains an independent research and engineering project. This dated note brings the public project summary forward; it is not a claim that the application or every workstream is finished.

Recent engineering work tightened how research inputs and evidence are handled: declared forecast windows are respected, time-bounded context is filtered by its relevant timestamps, and missing required market-state inputs remain explicitly unavailable rather than being presented as observed facts. Focused checks were completed for those scoped changes.

These improvements make the research process more careful and auditable. They do not establish profitable strategies, causal learning uplift, or full product acceptance.

## Current boundaries

- The MIOIQ application and trading runtime remain private; this repository publishes documentation and selected evidence, not a downloadable product.
- There is no open public demo at this time.
- Full current WebUI/product acceptance, complete end-to-end release validation, and an independently matched after-cost improvement remain open.
- Live trading is disabled, real capital is 0, and automatic promotion is disabled.

The public record is intentionally explicit about what is verified and what still needs evidence. See [Current status](../CURRENT_STATUS.md), [Public Repository Scope](../docs/project/PUBLIC_REPOSITORY_SCOPE.md) and [Evidence Summary](../evidence/EVIDENCE_SUMMARY.md).

Research and engineering only. No trading signals or investment advice.
