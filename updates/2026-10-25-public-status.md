# MIOIQ — Weekend research update (25 October 2026)

Deutsch: [Deutsche Fassung](2026-10-25-public-status_DE.md)

This is a reviewed summary of work since the [24 October update](2026-10-24-public-status.md). It describes specific checks and observations, not a completed release.

## What moved forward

The private research workflow now has more concrete evidence across crypto, stock and resource instruments. In one bounded check, the canonical reader found complete, quality-marked futures rows for all 22 configured instruments at the examined cutoff. A later check found only 18 of 22 within the required freshness window. This is useful progress on coverage, but it is **not** proof of continuously fresh data or of a trade-ready signal.

The current Paper workflow was also observed making normal research decisions for selected instruments when its inputs were usable. Some decisions ended in no trade. No new Paper entry, profitable strategy or economic improvement is established by these observations.

The private WebUI received a scoped visual check of Paper trade details. The inspected view showed historical closed trades, separated cost information and linked decision/outcome details. That check does not cover every page or make the application a public demo.

On the AI-analysis path, a later normal cycle produced recorded decisions for the available crypto subset. Other configured instruments were excluded when their required inputs were stale. A recorded decision is not an executed trade.

## What remains open

Reliable fresh-data publication and the complete producer-to-reader-to-decision chain still need sustained evidence. The new health-report coverage has source-level checks, but its loaded operation and end-to-end effect have not yet been accepted. A Paper outcome's later reassessment remains open. The ML hypothesis producer is paused by the operator; no current training or automatic model promotion is claimed.

The application and trading runtime remain private. There is no open public demo or download. Full WebUI/product acceptance, connected Demo/Testnet execution and a matched after-cost comparison showing causal improvement remain unproven. Live trading is disabled; real capital is **0**; automatic promotion is disabled.

We publish these limits because “data was present once,” “a decision was recorded,” and “a system is ready for users” are different claims. The next useful milestone is a repeatable, time-aligned record from fresh input through a natural Paper decision and its later outcome.
