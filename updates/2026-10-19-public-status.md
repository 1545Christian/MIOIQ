**Source and cutoff:** user-supplied MIOIQ master workbook, with its completeness audit updated 18 October 2026, 19:12 UTC and later 18 October workstream notes. This is a documentation reconciliation of reported/recorded evidence, **not a new private-code or live-product test**.

## What actually progressed in the code

**Independent AI analysis.** The canonical master marks final AI decision ownership, local monitoring of open AI positions without routine repeated paid prompts, suppression of duplicate provider calls, corrected historical outcome reconstruction, and analyst schema validation as PROVEN within their respective scopes. Recent October 18 records add provider ordering/capacity evidence and a source correction for invalid attempted price levels. However, the complete decision-to-order/outcome consumer chain, comprehensive cost reporting and after-cost quality are still PARTIAL or NOT_COMPARABLE. Historical and current performance periods must not be compared as if matched.

**Canonical outcome and learning evidence.** A production-write receipt and a subsequent read-only reconciliation report 256 outcome-linked recovery records with 256 corresponding links, contributions and learning deltas across separated evidence lanes. The parallel memory-outcome mirror covered only 216 of those 256 rows. That remaining 40-row gap and unresolved full decision-to-trade lineage keep the affected canonical-memory items PARTIAL; the receipt is not a clean end-to-end close.

**Strategy Workshop and Strategy Trades — newer owner evidence.** The two user-facing areas have now been separated and reportedly delivered in the regular WebUI build. The owner verified search, pagination, distinct CURRENT/V2 views and existing backtest results in the authenticated internal browser. The workshop catalogue exposes 1,616 pre-existing strategy records while retaining their original identities and trade links. Canvas connections, compatible-port highlighting, rule-summary synchronization and parameter edits were also exercised in a served build; the draft was restored without saving a new strategy. Later Learning safeguards prevent unbounded queries and automatic retries when the required index is missing. These are meaningful scoped source/build/browser improvements, not full completion of the two product concepts. The production Learning index and successful Learning browser cycle still lack an explicitly authorized maintenance window and verified capacity. Broader regression, end-to-end Paper/learning, permissions and real-user accessibility acceptance remain open; no trading positions or workers were changed by the reported UI work.

**Trade Like Che / Paper research.** Per-version private watch-minute producer, monitoring/decision/summary and transactional evidence, plus a Paper Arena verifier, have bounded source and isolated verification. Some focused tests passed; seven regression failures were separately reported without an accepted baseline. Source proof is not product, economic or global-ML proof. Natural Paper execution/settlement, fair V2-versus-V1 comparison and the complete trade/filter display still need acceptance.

**Runtime and market-data boundaries.** Focused service/process evidence exists, including a 9/9 singleton snapshot reported for 18 October. This does not prove start/stop/restart, fresh-data continuity or an independent recovery cycle. A later incident explicitly reopens questions of loader ownership, writer/read access and authoritative data identity. They must be resolved with the dedicated data-provider owner; do not silently introduce a second loader or assume the supervisor owns the external market feed.

**WebUI.** A current browser/source scope has progressed, but multiple trading-lane displays, exact filter counts, refresh consistency and the full current-truth acceptance remain PARTIAL or OPEN. A served build is not sufficient without a browser-level acceptance result.

**Storage, costs and research ML.** Storage compaction, evidence preservation and bounded cost-contract work have progressed; physical shrink, growth root cause and complete natural spread/slippage/funding/fill economics are not closed. No independently validated Challenger, proven causal learning uplift or matched after-cost model superiority is claimed.

**Product design.** Multi-venue, user settings/universe and future multi-asset requirements are being specified separately. Some functionality is source-level or deferred; broad product/venue support is not claimed active.
