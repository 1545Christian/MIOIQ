# MIOIQ public update — 2026-10-12

Deutsch: [Deutsches Update](2026-10-12-public-status_DE.md)

The work since the October 7 update has been about something less visible than a new feature: checking whether research, decisions, learning and recovery still behave correctly when the system is actually running.

## Runtime and recovery

A newer focused startup/recovery preflight passed at its tested boundary. Full recovery and whole-product acceptance still require natural runtime evidence across the relevant services. A preflight PASS is not a claim that the full system is release-ready.

## Paper V2 and learning

Paper V2 continues to produce decision evidence, but the latest reviewed population did not provide a valid new setup that could complete a fresh execution and settled outcome chain.

The system keeps those NO_TRADE / no-current-setup outcomes instead of forcing trades to demonstrate activity.

The Canonical Memory / Learning path has made further technical progress, including focused checks and exact links between settled outcomes and learning evidence. The key question remains open: does a natural outcome change a later comparable decision and produce a better result after costs?

That causal economic improvement is **not proven**.

## Research and ML

There is no new validated Challenger or model promotion to announce.

A credible comparison still needs aligned observation populations, independent out-of-sample evaluation, appropriate trial control and comparable after-cost outcomes. Training is not started merely to manufacture a success indicator.

## External AI analysis

The latest reviewed bounded provider evidence returned valid NO_TRADE decisions. That establishes scoped decision-path behavior, not profitable trading or better model quality.

## Storage and database

Future-write and evidence-preservation work continues. The database has nevertheless grown after earlier logical compaction. Physical shrink, the current growth source and query performance remain separate open engineering questions; no data-loss-risking cleanup is claimed complete.

## Demo and release

A previously tested invite/registration flow does not establish Connected Demo execution readiness.

End-to-end trade-to-learning evidence, recovery, current WebUI acceptance, source/manifest alignment and distribution checks remain open or partial. Technical proof and human product acceptance remain separate.

## What has not changed

- Live trading remains disabled; real capital remains zero.
- Automatic promotion remains disabled.
- V2 superiority, causal learning uplift and external AI profitability remain unproven.
- Complete execution-cost evidence, Connected Demo/Testnet E2E and release readiness remain open.

MIOIQ is being developed toward configurable multi-market analysis, ML research and evidence-driven adaptation. The point is not to generate more trades — it is to learn what works, under which conditions, and when **NO_TRADE** is the better decision.

**Research → Evidence → Validation → Controlled Execution**
