# MIOIQ — Research & Engineering Update

**16 October 2026** · Reviewed tracker checkpoint: **15 October 2026**

Deutsch: [German edition](2026-10-16-public-status_DE.md)

Since the last update, work has continued on runtime recovery, Paper V2, learning, research validation, the WebUI and longer-term product architecture.

## Runtime and market data

Focused preflight and recovery work has progressed. Complete independent startup, fresh-data and recovery acceptance still needs a joined-up forward check. A running process alone is not proof that every research path is functioning with current data.

## Paper V2 and learning

The reviewed Paper V2 population still lacks a new eligible execution and settlement. No trades are forced simply to produce activity.

Canonical Memory and learning have stronger links and focused verification. The remaining question is whether a naturally settled outcome changes a later comparable decision and improves its result after costs. That economic effect is not yet established.

## ML and research

An independently validated Challenger remains pending. Fair out-of-sample comparison, eligible samples, cost-aware evaluation and lifecycle evidence are still required. Incomplete or rejected candidates are not promoted.

## External AI research

The reviewed provider cycles include valid NO_TRADE decisions. That is technical evidence of completed analysis, not a claim of profitable trading.

## WebUI and Demo

Decision display and usability work continued. However, refresh consistency, current browser acceptance and complete product parity remain open.

The previously tested registration/access path is separate from Connected Demo execution and recovery, which are not yet fully accepted.

## Storage and cost evidence

Evidence-preserving storage improvements have progressed, but measured database growth and physical shrink remain unresolved. Likewise, a tested cost-handling contract does not establish complete observed execution economics.

## Future scope

MIOIQ's longer-term planning includes configurable market coverage, multi-venue operation and a broader multi-asset direction. Eligibility and permissions for markets and users are being specified. These are **future design areas**, not launched capabilities.

## Project acceptance

The tracker contains **320 master items**. Technical progress and human acceptance are deliberately separate; the current checkpoint still has **no final product acceptance**.

The Demo Learning Effectiveness milestone remains **IN_PROGRESS**. Natural Paper V2 outcomes, later learning impact, independent Challenger evidence, Connected Demo execution and release integrity remain key gates.

**Live trading disabled · Real capital 0 · Automatic promotion disabled**

MIOIQ's goal remains configurable market analysis, machine-learning research and evidence-driven adaptation — learning not only what works, but also when **NO_TRADE** is appropriate.

**Research → Evidence → Validation → Controlled Execution**
